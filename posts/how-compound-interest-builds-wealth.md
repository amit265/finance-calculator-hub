# How Compound Interest Builds Wealth

Compound interest is often referred to as the "eighth wonder of the world." For those who understand it, it's a powerful tool for building wealth. For those who don't, it can be a costly burden.

## What is Compound Interest?

In simple terms, compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal, which also includes all of the accumulated interest from previous periods. Unlike simple interest, which is only calculated on the principal amount, compound interest allows your money to grow exponentially.

## The Power of Time

The most critical factor in compounding is time. The longer your money has to grow, the more dramatic the results. This is why financial advisors constantly stress the importance of starting early.

### An Example

Imagine you invest $10,000 at an annual interest rate of 7%.
- After 10 years, you would have approximately $19,671.
- After 20 years, you would have approximately $38,696.
- After 30 years, you would have approximately $76,122.

As you can see, the growth in the final 10 years is much larger than the growth in the first 10 years. This is the exponential nature of compounding.

## Consistency is Key

While starting early is ideal, consistency is equally important. Regularly adding to your investment—even small amounts—can significantly boost the final result.

## Conclusion

Understanding compound interest is the first step toward financial independence. By starting early and staying consistent, you can harness this mathematical force to secure your financial future.
